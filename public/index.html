OUR EDITORIAL PROMISE

Trust the method.
Question the verdict.

One methodology for every outlet, ideology and writer. Readers can inspect and disagree with the evidence without being told what to believe.

01

Facts are not opinions

Specific, testable claims can be checked. Values, predictions and political opinions are labelled as such instead of being misrepresented as true or false.

02

One claim, one evidence trail

Every supported or contradicted verdict should show the exact claim, relevant date, independently inspectable source, limitations and review history. Search results and existing fact-check verdicts are leads—not proof by themselves.

03

Separate the six dimensions

Claim accuracy, sourcing, missing context, language, framing and author disclosures are shown independently. Political affiliation is recorded only where documented, not inferred from style or identity.

04

We can say "we don't know"

When the original article or independent evidence cannot be obtained, an overall numerical score is withheld. Coverage and uncertainty are always visible.

05

No ideological penalty

We do not penalise political positions. Sensational framing can be identified in any publication. Documented bias in reporting is not the same thing as a false factual claim.

06

Correct the record, publicly

For a production launch, reviewed assessments must retain evidence snapshots, reviewer identity, version history, appeals and visible corrections. This prototype does not pretend to provide editorial sign-off.

WHY NO AUTOMATIC 82/100?

A precise-looking number can be misleading.

A composite score is only useful when dimensions have auditable evidence and the reviewed text adequately represents the article. Until then, Clarity shows Not assessed rather than inventing certainty. Scores should be published only through an accountable review process with an explicit weighting formula and transparent coverage.
